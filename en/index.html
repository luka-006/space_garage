Common questions

What does ambient lighting or a starlight headliner cost?

Ambient lighting is around €400 to €450, and a starlight headliner is €0.90 per star, so 300 stars come to €270. We confirm the exact price once you send the make and model. Asking costs nothing.

What is a starlight headliner?

Fibre optics fitted into the car roof that glow like stars. It can be dense or just a few stars, and it can have a shooting star.

How long does a starlight headliner take to fit?

It depends on the car and the number of stars. We give you a rough time together with the price once you send the make and model.

Can I choose the colour?

You can. We fit ambient lighting and starlight headliners in the colour you pick. Try the colours at the top of this page and see how each one looks.

Do you do upholstery?

We do. A headliner is €250. Seats, steering wheels and trim in leather, alcantara or fabric are priced on request, with stitching in the colour you choose.

Do you fit Android Auto and CarPlay?

Yes. We fit screens with Android Auto and CarPlay, reversing cameras and front and rear parking sensors.

Do you come to my address?

We do. Minor servicing happens wherever your car is, at home or at work: oil changes, filters, fluid checks and small repairs. We cover Knin and the surrounding area, as far as Drniš. Weekday call-outs from 8am to 10pm are priced on request, and emergency night call-outs cost more.

When can I reach you?

Working hours are 8am to 10pm, and we answer the phone outside those hours too. For emergencies we also come out at night, at a higher rate. Call +385 98 136 3967 or send an enquiry through this page.
