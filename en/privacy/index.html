Cookies and privacy

The whole story, no small print.

Cookies

This site uses no cookies. We do not set them, we do not read them, and we have no tool that would. That is why there is no banner asking you to accept anything.

Tracking and analytics

There is no Google Analytics or any other visitor measurement. We do not know who visited, how long they stayed or where they came from.

External content

Fonts, images and video load from this site, not from Google or other services. The site is served by a hosting provider which, like any web server, logs technical data about visits, such as IP addresses, for security and to keep the site running.

Data you send us

The form on the site stores nothing. When you press send, a message opens in your own app with your choices filled in, and you decide whether to send it. We only see it once you do.

If you contact us by message or phone, we keep only what you wrote yourself: your number, name, car details and what we agreed. We use it to reply and do the work. We never pass it on or sell it.

Outbound links

The WhatsApp and Instagram buttons lead to those services. From the moment you open them their rules apply, not ours.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it. Call +385 98 136 3967 and it is done.

If you think your rights were not respected, you can contact the Croatian Data Protection Agency (azop.hr) or your local authority.
